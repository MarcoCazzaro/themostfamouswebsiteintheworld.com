# The Most Famous Website In The World

A social ranking platform where anyone can become famous — or make their favourite celebrities even more famous.

## What is it?

The Most Famous Website In The World is a place where users are ranked by popularity through **famous points**. The more famous points you collect, the higher you climb in the rankings.

## How do famous points work?

Every time someone visits your profile page, a progress bar fills up at the bottom of the screen. When it reaches 100%, you earn one famous point. The longer a visitor stays on your page, the more points they give you — so loyal fans really matter.

## What can you do here?

- **Become famous** — sign up, build your profile and let people visit your page to earn points.
- **Support your favourites** — search for famous people you admire and stay on their profile to give them points.
- **Climb the rankings** — compete in the global **Most Famous People** leaderboard or the **Most Famous Fans** leaderboard (rewarding those who give the most points to others).
- **Get discovered by category** — add up to 5 tags to your profile (e.g. Music, Sports, Gaming) to appear in tag-specific rankings on the **Most Famous Tags** page.

## Rankings

| Ranking | Who appears |
|---|---|
| Most Famous People | Users with the most famous points received |
| Most Famous Fans | Users who have given the most famous points to others |
| Most Famous Tags | The most popular interest categories |

## Getting started

1. Create an account and verify your email.
2. Complete your profile — add a bio, social links and up to 5 tags.
3. Share your profile link so people can visit and give you famous points.
4. Visit the profiles of people you follow to support them.
